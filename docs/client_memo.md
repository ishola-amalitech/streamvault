**Recommendation.** Start with standalone films in both windows.

Across all years, the typical standalone scored **8.36** versus **7.59** for a bundle, based on 16 standalones and 32 bundles. From 2000 onward, the scores were **8.14** and **7.15**, based on 8 standalones and 24 bundles. Standalones still led after setting aside the strongest film in each bundle. Scores are out of 10 and give less weight to ratings with fewer votes.

**Talent.** Christopher Nolan’s films stayed ahead of similar titles in both windows after setting aside his biggest series. Harrison Ford showed a positive pattern across all years and Michael Caine did from 2000 onward. Cast alone gives little basis for paying more.

**Deal note · The Godfather trilogy.** Ask for the first all-years bundle quote. The deciding number is **8.47**, the middle score of its three films. Its narrow lead over *The Lord of the Rings* at 8.40 warrants little premium, especially with *Part III* at 7.36. At comparable prices, prioritize *The Shawshank Redemption* at 8.68. If Finance uses 2000 onward, give *The Lord of the Rings* the first bundle call.

**What we couldn’t verify**

- **Licensing prices.** We have no studio quotes, so we can’t say which deals fit the $14M.
- **Viewing and returns.** Ratings and theater receipts tell us how films did elsewhere. We lack StreamVault viewing data to forecast demand or returns, or justify paying more for a star.
- **Bundle contents.** Hannibal’s score comes from *The Silence of the Lambs* alone. Ask each studio which films come with its offer before judging the whole package.
- **Missing candidates.** We scored 112 distinct films from 127 entries. Six entries were repeats and one was another ID for *The Dark Knight*, which we already scored. The other eight entries could contain stronger picks or change the comparison.
- **Historical box office.** Franchise films typically earned more in theaters in both windows. We couldn’t verify the effects of inflation or currency differences. Use those figures as context. The case for calling standalones first rests on ratings.

Seek standalone quotes first in either window. Confirm bundle titles and get prices before committing any of the $14M.
